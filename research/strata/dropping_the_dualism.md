# Dropping the Dualism

```
Forces                    instead of        Reality              or        Mind
   ↕                                           ↓                             ↓
Interactions                              Representation                   World
   ↕
Emergence
```

Both alternatives are **dualisms with a one-way arrow and a gap**. This project has been built
on the first — it is in the name. "Universal **Representation** for Adaptive Systems" presumes
a reality the model corresponds to, and correspondence must be unique, which is exactly where
determinacy came from as a goal rather than as an instrument.

---

## The retrodiction

The strongest evidence for a frame is that it explains a failure the previous frame could not.

**The worst unfixed measurement in this project:** estimand agreement of 0.07–0.17 on frontier
systems. Gemini produced three doctrine-level estimands for Toyota — `process_is_optimal`,
`report_matches_reality`, `defect_present`. DeepSeek produced six operational ones —
`station_cycle_time`, `defect_rate`, `line_stoppage_frequency`, `annual_production_target`.

I treated this as a determinacy defect and attacked it twice:

1. The settling-observation individuation rule. Effect: **0.122 → 0.111.** None.
2. Anchoring estimands to declared loops. Effect: **0.111 → 0.000.** Worse.

Under a stratified ontology those encoders are not disagreeing. They are describing **different
levels of the same system**, and both are right. The flat schema forced them into one list and
the metric scored the difference as noise.

**The failure was in the frame, not the encoders.** Two rules failed because the problem was
not rule-shaped.

## The mapping is cleaner than it should be

All 19 primitives place, none left over:

| level | primitives |
|---|---|
| **Forces** — what pushes | Constraint, Resource, DesiredCondition, PreferenceOrdering, Consequence, Delay, TimeScale |
| **Interactions** — what happens between | Signal, Estimator, Estimate, Intervention, Policy, Loop, Calibration, Estimand |
| **Emergence** — what arises | System, Boundary, Party |
| **Cross-level** | Revision |

`Revision` landing alone as the cross-level operator is the useful part. It is **downward
causation** — an emergent identity changing the forces that produce it — which is precisely
why it never fit as either a parameter update or an ordinary intervention, and why the
refuted/abandoned distinction mattered so much.

## It delivers a claim that was made and abandoned

`research/prior_art_gate.md` argued that URAS differs from every POMDP variant because **the
observer is inside the system** — second-order cybernetics, Beer's recursion, the thing HRL
cannot express. That claim justified the whole project and was then never built.

Under `Mind → World`, a `Party` is a subject looking at an object: outside, representing.
Under stratification a `Party` is **emergent from interactions and exerts force back on them**.
It is in the system. That is the claim, made structural.

## Two cautions, recorded before adoption

**It is not globally MECE.** A price is emergent from trades and a force on traders.
Temperature is emergent from molecular motion and a force on a thermostat. A boundary is
emergent from a system's operation and a force on what it can observe. The scheme is MECE
*within a level*, with the same entity recurring across levels in different roles.

That is either the deep insight — multi-scale systems genuinely work this way — or a licence
for ambiguity that will make encodings less comparable, not more. It cannot be both, and only
measurement distinguishes them.

**It is substantially prior art.** Bhaskar's critical realism is a stratified ontology with
emergence and downward causation in almost these terms. Beer's Viable System Model is recursive
levels. Simon's near-decomposability is the same intuition about scale separation. The
Deleuzian assemblage reading in `external_critiques.md` was already pointing here and was
recorded as "not acted on."

None of that is an objection. The charter's Phase 1 instruction was to survey adjacent work and
identify what is missing rather than to copy it — so the honest question is what this adds to
Bhaskar and Beer, and the current answer is: an executable target and a measurement discipline,
not a new metaphysics.

## The test

Adopted only if it earns it, on the same instrument everything else here faced.

A sixth arm in the reasoning experiment: build a **stratified** model from the same prose, with
explicit upward and downward couplings, and answer the same four questions. It receives no more
material than the `generated` arm — only a different organising schema — so anything it wins is
attributable to stratification.

**Prediction, recorded before the result:** stratification should help most on the questions
that cross levels — how a local action produces a system-level failure (Q2), and why insiders
cannot resolve a disagreement (Q3, which is often a level confusion rather than an evidential
gap). It should help least on Q1, which is mostly local.

If it does not beat the flat generated model, it is an appealing diagram and should be recorded
as one.
