# Pre-registered — batch of 17, written BEFORE any archive is opened

Committed before fetching. The n=2 result was limited by both cases being developer
infrastructure; this batch spans data infra, AI hardware, privacy tech, fusion and
semiconductors specifically to test whether the pattern is market-specific.

## The claim under test

From the two-case result:

> An ambitious general abstraction lost to a narrow, concrete primitive with an incremental
> adoption path.

## Predictions

**P1 — Base rate (~55%).** Among companies with a usable before/after, a **majority** of
SOFTWARE-INFRASTRUCTURE ones will show narrowing: the headline moves from a broad
paradigm/platform claim toward a specific, concrete capability. Counted as narrowing only if
the *category noun* changes toward something more specific, or an ideological term is dropped.

**P2 — Domain split (~65%).** Narrowing will be **markedly less common** in
hardware/deep-tech — proxima fusion, ncodin, fractile, positron, lumai — because there is no
abstraction layer to narrow. Their positioning should stay stable or broaden as capability
matures. If the rate is the same in both groups, the pattern is about company maturation
generally, not about abstractions, and the URAS reading collapses.

**P3 — Incremental adoption (~40%).** Among the narrowing cases, a substantial minority will
explicitly adopt incremental-entry language — "drop-in", "one route at a time", "brownfield",
"no migration", "works with your existing X". This is the specific mechanism from ElectricSQL
and it is the one URAS lacks entirely.

**P4 — Unusable rate (~30%).** Roughly a third will have no usable baseline — parked domains,
too few snapshots, or founded too recently — as happened with Lodestar.

**P5 — Agent drift (~35%).** At least a third of software cases will show AI-agent language
appearing by 2025-26 regardless of original category. If this is near-universal it supports
the counter-explanation that xorq's pivot was market timing rather than a truth about
abstractions.

## Scoring rules, fixed in advance

- "Narrowing" requires the specific change named, not merely different wording.
- A case with fewer than two usable 200-status snapshots at least 9 months apart is UNUSABLE
  and is excluded from rates rather than counted either way.
- Rates are computed over usable cases only, and the usable count is reported.
- Where I already know a company well (supabase, databricks, neon, motherduck) that is noted,
  since those are not blind.
