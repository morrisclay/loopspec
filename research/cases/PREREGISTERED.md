# Pre-registered predictions — written BEFORE opening any archive

The xorq case had a fatal selection flaw: it was chosen knowing the company had repositioned.
These two are chosen without knowing, and the predictions below are timestamped by the commit
that adds them, before any snapshot is fetched.

## Case 2 — ElectricSQL

**What I know now:** local-first sync engine for Postgres. Noted earlier in this project as a
system where "correctness is provable, adoption is not" — two radically different estimand
types in one company.

**Prediction A (primary, ~55%).** Positioning narrows from a broad local-first/offline
platform toward a specific, concrete capability — most likely **Postgres → client sync as
plumbing** rather than as an application paradigm. The "local-first" ideology recedes; the
sync primitive stays.

**Prediction B (~30%).** A shift toward AI agents, matching xorq — read paths for agents,
or sync as agent state.

**Prediction C (~15%).** No material change. Same thesis, refined.

**What would refute the URAS-relevant claim:** if ElectricSQL moved *toward* portability and
engine-independence rather than away, the xorq finding does not generalise.

## Case 3 — Lodestar Space

**What I know now:** London, founded 2023, space operations technology. ~$2.8M equity against
~$3.6M in grants and contracts from UK Space Agency, ESA and UK MoD — more public money than
private.

**Prediction A (primary, ~50%).** Positioning shifts toward **defence and sovereign capability
framing** and away from commercial space services, following where the money actually is.

**Prediction B (~30%).** Narrowing from a broad "space operations" platform to one specific
mission or product.

**Prediction C (~20%).** Little change — hardware timelines are long and 2-3 years is not
enough for a public repositioning.

## The cross-case question

xorq moved from **portability** to **provenance**. That was read as evidence against URAS's
SQL analogy.

**Prediction:** the portability→provenance pattern will NOT appear in Lodestar, because it is
a hardware and mission company with no abstraction layer to reposition. If the pattern appears
in ElectricSQL too, it is about software-infrastructure abstractions specifically, which is
the class URAS belongs to. If it appears in neither, the xorq reading was over-fitted to one
company.

**Scoring rule, fixed in advance:** a prediction counts as correct only if the archive shows
the specific shift named, not merely "something changed."
