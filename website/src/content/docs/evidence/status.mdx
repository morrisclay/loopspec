LoopSpec v1.1 remains readable and locally verified. The current v1.2 / IR v2.2 producer adds an
experimental control plane, but its bounded independent-encoding study stopped below the
preregistered convergence threshold. That negative result is retained rather than promoted away.

## Verified locally

- every one of the 97 accepted authoring fields has a documented semantic effect;
- source input flows through fail-closed authoring validation, expansion, IR validation, and
  analysis;
- all nine shipped product fixtures have pinned normalized semantic and finding-set hashes;
- every active authoring check has an executable positive case;
- a complete design is a shared zero-finding negative;
- safety-relevant mutations expose omitted approval, reversibility, process, calibration,
  resource, and reference semantics;
- map ordering and Python hash seed do not alter semantics or findings;
- the complete source gate passes on Python 3.11 through 3.14;
- the installable wheel passes check, diff, and runtime doctor outside the source checkout.
- the real-loop gate validates 25 encodings, including 7 cases pinned to exact upstream Git
  revisions across coding, browser use, and autonomous research.

## Evidence supporting particular checks

The strongest corpus result is narrow: `belief_never_checked` and
`no_explicit_process_model` survived an independent encoding exercise across the held-out
published examples. Other checks are labelled `corpus` or `motivated` rather than borrowing
that robustness.

A separate 2026 runtime replication recovered every primary finding category for Browser Use
but only 2 of 6 categories for SWE-agent. Source-level causal omissions agreed more often than
their graph projections. This rejects defect-rate claims from raw linter counts and motivates
the [real-loop convergence work](/evidence/real-loops/).

Prevalence and assurance are kept separate. A finding can be structurally certain about the
spec while its real-world usefulness remains unevidenced.

## Open evidence gates

1. An independently developed implementation must produce the same canonical IR for the
   normative fixtures.
2. At least twelve external loop authors across at least three domains must correct their
   encodings and adjudicate the findings.
3. The preregistered precision, encoding-error, action-rate, and leave-one-group-out thresholds
   must pass before the claim advances to “useful design review tool.”
4. Acted-on cases require later outcome collection before any prospective-value claim.

<Aside type="caution" title="Not established">
LoopSpec does not prove dynamic stability, observability, controllability, requisite variety,
runtime enforcement, incident prevention, or improved production outcomes. It makes relevant
structural premises explicit so stronger tools can know when those questions are even well
formed.
</Aside>

## The contribution, stated narrowly

The credible contribution is an executable specification discipline that places operating
feedback, attention, calibration, governance, provenance, reversibility, and viability in one
reviewable artifact—and refuses to translate structural proxies into control-theoretic proofs.
